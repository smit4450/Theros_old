* Frost Goliath World Tree Barbarian
* Comes from a Haugbrud (hill goliath) tribe in the mountain range called Skorvag's Crown.
* Her mother went to fight with the goliath war bands in Roderic's rebellion and returned pregnant with Ingrid.
	* Ingrid is larger and paler than most of her kin due to Isebrud (frost goliath) blood.
* Due in part to her heritage, Ingrid has struggled with her temper her entire life. This came to a head when she destroyed a small halfling settlement during a game of tag with the local children.
	* In response, her family performed a druidic rite to tattoo her with Aardtree sap from their sacred tree.
	* The tattoos help her control her rage. Most of the time they are a faint blue-white, but they get darker as her emotions rise, until they turn black.
	* She has always felt a connection to her family's sacred Aardtree.
* Recently, the empire has turned it's eyes on the Aardenvale (the valley within Skorvag's Crown), and began disrupting the halfling and goliath inhabitants.
