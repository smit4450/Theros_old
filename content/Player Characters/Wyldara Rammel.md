* High elf college of eloquence bard from Rath Borea in the north.
* Father is the elvish ambassador in Rath Borea from Falindor, a small wealthy nation, where he was a member of high society.
	* Being sent to a backwater kingdom such as Rath Borea is considered an insult and punishment.
* Wyldara was raised wealthy, spoiled, but ignored, as the fifth child and third daughter of her house. Her father is a cruel lord, her mother an entitled self-centered lady. Wyldara ran away in her teens, but found a harsh world outside the aristocracy. Perhaps a little naive, she made a deal with the goddess Maiv in order to gain the capacity for self-reliance. She received a boon of incredible musical talent, however, she now owes more than a simple favor to the goddess of bargains. She has made her own way as a court musician, trading in secrets and favors.
