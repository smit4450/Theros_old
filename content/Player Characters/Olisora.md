* Dragonborn warlock.
* Many dragonborn have a tradition of abandoning their young to fend for themselves.
* Olisora was found by a traveling storyteller who raised her as his own. She learned how to get by with little resources, helping her father scrape by as they travelled the lands. In many places she was an outcast due to her people's reputation. Her father taught her kindness and empathy, even for the ignorance of many. He also taught and showed her many cultures and myths. Eventually, the old man passed away. Every since, Olisora has been doing her best to continue his legacy and bring knowledge to those she can. But not everyone is welcoming.
