Ril is a world that exists in the [Material Plane](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Prime_Material_plane), although it features connections to other [[planes]] of existence, as well as other worlds.

## [[History]]

Since the creation of Ril, there have been several distinct eras: The [[Age of Ancients]], [[The  Cataclysmic Age]], followed by the [[The First Age|First]], [[The Second Age|Second]], and [[The Third Age|Third]] ages. The events of the campaign take place in the Third Age. See [[Timeline]].

## Deities
Most peoples of Ril worship a selection of gods from the major pantheon:
* [[Ord]]: God of order, achievement, and self-actualization. Younger of the Jotun brothers.
* [[Aard]]: Dead god of ...
* [[The Básidhe]]: Twin aspect deity of opposites, bargains, and fate.
* [[Lady Ley]]: Goddess of arcane magic.
* [[Pyra]]: Dragon goddess of fire and passion.
* [[Myrk]]: Dragon god of darkness and the void.
* [[The Wanderer]]: Nameless god of travelers, secrets, and stories.

* [[Aurelianus]]: Controversial god-emperor of the Aurelian Empire.
