- **Feat.**  [[Skilled]]  
- **Skill Proficiencies.** [Athletics](Compendium/rules/skills.md#Athletics) and [Sleight of Hand](Compendium/rules/skills.md#Sleight of Hand)

- **Equipment.** Choose A or B: (A) [[Dagger]], [[Weaver's Tools]], [[Backpack]], [[Ball Bearings]], [[Basket]], [[Bedroll]], [[Bucket]], [[Rations]] (3 days' worth), [[Rope]], [[Signal Whistle]], [[Traveler's Clothes]], 38 GP; or (B) 50 GP  

Though most Lektoi in Akros accept their obligation of military service, you bristled at that authoritarian attempt to control your life. You forsook your nationhood, discarded your given name, and worked as a freebooter with the first ship that would have you. Since then, you've traveled the Dakra Isles. Though you've never sailed more than a few dozen leagues from land, you make up for it with deep local connections and the breadth of your experiences.
