# Spellfire Initiate
*Source: FRHoF p. 36*  
![](Compendium/backgrounds/img/spellfire-initiate.webp#right)

- **Ability Scores.** Constitution, Intelligence, Charisma  
- **Feat.**  [[Spellfire Spark]]  
- **Skill Proficiencies.** [Arcana](Skills.md#Arcana) and [Perception](Skills.md#Perception)  
- **Tool Proficiencies.** Choose one kind of [[Gaming Set]]  
- **Equipment.** Choose A or B: (A) [[Gaming Set]] (same as above), [[Arcane Focus]] ([[Crystal]] or [[Wand]]), 2 [[Pouch]], [[Traveler's Clothes]], 36 GP; or (B) 50 GP  

You bear the gift of spellfire: a rare form of magic that channels the raw power of the Weave. Wielding spellfire takes a heavy toll on the body. You've trained both mind and body to efficiently wield this sacred power.
