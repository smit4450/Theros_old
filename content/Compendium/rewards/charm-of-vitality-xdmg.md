# Charm of Vitality

This Charm allows you to give yourself the benefit of a [[Potion of Vitality]] as a [Magic](Actions.md#Magic) action. Once you do so, the Charm vanishes from you.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 99*
