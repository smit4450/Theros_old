# Challenge Rating
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 363. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)* 

Challenge Rating (CR) summarizes the threat a [[monster-xphb]] poses to a group of four [[player-character-xphb]]. Compare a [[monster-xphb]] CR to the characters' level. If the CR is higher, the [[monster-xphb]] is likely a danger. If the CR is lower, the [[monster-xphb]] likely poses little threat. But circumstances and the number of [[player-character-xphb]] can significantly alter how threatening a [[monster-xphb]] is in actual play. The "Dungeon Master's Guide" provides guidance to the DM on using CR while planning potential combat [[encounter-xphb]].
