# Hit Point Dice
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 368. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)* 

Hit Point Dice, or Hit Dice for short, help determine a [[Player Character]] Hit Point maximum, as explained in "chapter 2". Most [[Monster]] also have Hit Dice. A creature can spend Hit Dice during a [[Short Rest]] to regain [[Hit Points]].
