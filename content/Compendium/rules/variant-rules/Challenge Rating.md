# Challenge Rating
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 363. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)* 

Challenge Rating (CR) summarizes the threat a [[Monster]] poses to a group of four [[Player Character]]. Compare a [[Monster]] CR to the characters' level. If the CR is higher, the [[Monster]] is likely a danger. If the CR is lower, the [[Monster]] likely poses little threat. But circumstances and the number of [[Player Character]] can significantly alter how threatening a [[Monster]] is in actual play. The "Dungeon Master's Guide" provides guidance to the DM on using CR while planning potential combat [[Encounter]].
