# Heavy Precipitation
*Environmental Hazard*  

Everything within an area of heavy rain or heavy snowfall is [[Lightly Obscured]], and creatures in the area have [[Disadvantage]] on all Wisdom ([Perception](Skills.md#Perception)) checks. Heavy rain also extinguishes open flames.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 69*
