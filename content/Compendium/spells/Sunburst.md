# Sunburst
*8th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a piece of sunstone)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Brilliant sunlight flashes in a 60-foot-radius [[Sphere (Area of Effect)]] centered on a point you choose within range. Each creature in the [[Sphere (Area of Effect)]] makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes `12d6` Radiant damage and has the [Blinded](Conditions.md#Blinded) condition for 1 minute. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage only.

A creature [Blinded](Conditions.md#Blinded) by this spell makes another Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

This spell dispels [[Compendium/spells/Darkness]] in its area that was created by any spell.
