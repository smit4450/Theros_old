# Power Word Heal
*9th-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A wave of healing energy washes over one creature you can see within range. The target regains all its [[Hit Points]]. If the creature has the [Charmed](Conditions.md#Charmed), [Frightened](Conditions.md#Frightened), [Paralyzed](Conditions.md#Paralyzed), [Poisoned](Conditions.md#Poisoned), or [Stunned](Conditions.md#Stunned) condition, the condition ends. If the creature has the [Prone](Conditions.md#Prone) condition, it can use its [[Reaction]] to stand up.

**Classes**: [[Bard Spells]]; [[Cleric Spells]]
