# Aura of Vitality
*3rd-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (30-foot Emanation)
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

An aura radiates from you in a 30-foot [[Emanation (Area of Effect)]] for the duration. When you create the aura and at the start of each of your turns while it persists, you can restore `2d6` [[Hit Points]] to one creature in it.
