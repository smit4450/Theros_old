# Destructive Wave
*5th-level, Evocation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (30-foot Sphere)
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

Destructive energy ripples outward from you in a 30-foot [[Emanation (Area of Effect)]]. Each creature you choose in the [[Emanation (Area of Effect)]] makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes `5d6` Thunder damage and `5d6` Radiant or Necrotic damage (your choice) and has the [Prone](Conditions.md#Prone) condition. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage only.

**Classes**: [[Paladin Spells]]

*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 261*
