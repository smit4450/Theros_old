# Stand Firm
*Source: FRHoF p. 38*  

You gain the following benefits.

**Stand as One.** When an ally within 5 feet of you is subjected to an effect that would push or pull it, you can take a [[Reaction]] to prevent that ally from being pushed or pulled. To receive this benefit, the ally can't have the [Incapacitated](Conditions.md#Incapacitated) condition.

**Vigilant.** When you take the [Ready](Actions.md#Ready) action, the next attack roll made against you has [[Disadvantage]] before the start of your next turn.
