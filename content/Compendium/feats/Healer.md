# Healer
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 201*  

You gain the following benefits.

**Battle Medic.** If you have a [[Healer's Kit]], you can expend one use of it and tend to a creature within 5 feet of yourself as a [Utilize](Compendium/rules/actions.md#Utilize) action. That creature can expend one of its [[hit-point-dice-xphb]], and you then roll that die. The creature regains a number of [[hit-points-xphb]] equal to the roll plus your [[proficiency-xphb]].

**Healing Rerolls.** Whenever you roll a die to determine the number of [[hit-points-xphb]] you restore with a spell or with this feat's Battle Medic benefit, you can reroll the die if it rolls a 1, and you must use the new roll.
