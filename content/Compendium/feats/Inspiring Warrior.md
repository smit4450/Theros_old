# Inspiring Warrior
*Source: FRHoF p. 38*  

You gain the following benefits.

**Inspiring Strike.** Once per turn when you score a [[critical-hit-xphb]] against a creature, you can choose an ally within 30 feet of yourself who can see or hear you and who lacks [[heroic-inspiration-xphb]]. That ally gains [[heroic-inspiration-xphb]].

**Reassert Honor.** When an enemy you can see deals damage to an ally of yours that is within 5 feet of you, you have [[advantage-xphb]] on your next attack roll against that enemy before the end of your next turn.
