# Boon of Terror
*Source: FRHoF p. 43*  

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Fearless.** You have [[Immunity]] to the [Frightened](Compendium/rules/conditions.md#Frightened) condition.

**Flee, Fools!.** When a creature with the [Frightened](Compendium/rules/conditions.md#Frightened) condition starts its turn within 60 feet of you, you can take a [[Reaction]] to stoke its terror, provided you can see the creature and it isn't behind [[cover-xphb]]. If you do so, the creature must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC 8 plus your Charisma modifier and your [[Proficiency]]) or spend its turn moving away from you by the fastest available means. Once you use this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a [[Short Rest]] or [[Long Rest]].

**Intimidating.** You gain [[Proficiency]] in the [Intimidation](Compendium/rules/skills.md#Intimidation) skill if you don't already have it. You also gain [[Expertise]] in [Intimidation](Compendium/rules/skills.md#Intimidation).
