# Iron Spike
*Adventuring gear*  


- **Cost**: 1 sp
- **Weight**: 0.5 lbs.

Iron Spikes come in bundles of ten. As a [Utilize](Actions.md#Utilize) action, you can use a blunt object, such as a [[Light Hammer]], to hammer a spike into wood, earth, or a similar material. You can do so to jam a door shut or to then tie a [[Rope]] or [[chain-xphb]] to the Spike.

*Source: Player's Handbook[Chain](Chain.md)title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
