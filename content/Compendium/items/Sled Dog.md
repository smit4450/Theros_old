# Sled Dog
*Mount*  


- **Cost**: 50 gp

*We recommend a team of these sled dogs to ease the burden on individual animals. Rotate your dogs through the lead positions to give weary animals a rest.*

A sled dog uses the [Wolf](Wolf.md)al). A sled dog pulling a sled alone must take a [[Short Rest]] after pulling a sled for 1 hour; otherwise, it gains 1 [Exhaustion](Conditions.md#Exhaustion) level.

*Source: FRHoF p. 132*
