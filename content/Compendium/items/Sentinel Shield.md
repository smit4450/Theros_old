# Sentinel Shield
*Armor ([[Compendium/items/Shield]]), uncommon*  


- **Armor Class**: 2
- **Weight**: 6.0 lbs.

While holding this Shield, you have [[Advantage]] on [[Initiative]] rolls and Wisdom ([Perception](Skills.md#Perception)) checks. The Shield is emblazoned with a symbol of an eye.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 303. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
