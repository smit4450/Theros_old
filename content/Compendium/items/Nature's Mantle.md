# Nature's Mantle
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement by a druid or ranger)*  
![](Compendium/items/img/natures-mantle.webp#right)


This cloak shifts color and texture to blend with the terrain surrounding you. While wearing the cloak, you can use it as a [[spellcasting-focus-xphb]] for your Druid and Ranger spells.

While you are in an area that is [[lightly-obscured-xphb]], you can [Hide](Compendium/rules/actions.md#Hide) as a [[bonus-action-xphb]] even if you are being directly observed.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 280*
