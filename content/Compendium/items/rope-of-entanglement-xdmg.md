# Rope of Entanglement
*Wondrous item, rare*  
![](Compendium/items/img/rope-of-entanglement.webp#right)


This rope is 30 feet long. While holding one end of the rope, you can take a [Magic](Compendium/rules/actions.md#Magic) action to command the other end to dart forward and entangle one creature you can see within 20 feet of yourself. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or have the [Restrained](Compendium/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) condition. You can release the target by letting go of your end of the rope (causing the rope to coil up in the target's space) or by using a [[bonus-action-xphb]] to repeat the command (causing the rope to coil up in your hand).

A target [Restrained](Compendium/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) by the rope can take an action to make its choice of a DC 15 Strength ([Athletics](Compendium/rules/skills.md#Athletics)) or Dexterity ([Acrobatics](Compendium/rules/skills.md#Acrobatics)) check. On a successful check, the target is no longer [Restrained](Compendium/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) by the rope. If you're still holding onto the rope when a target escapes from it, you can take a [[reaction-xphb]] to command the rope to coil up in your hand; otherwise, the rope coils up in the target's space.

The rope has AC 20, HP 20, and [[immunity-xphb]] to Poison and Psychic damage. It regains 1 [[hit-points-xphb]] every 5 minutes as long as it has at least 1 [[hit-points-xphb]]. If the rope drops to 0 [[hit-points-xphb]], it is destroyed.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 301. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
