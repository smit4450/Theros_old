# Necropoikos
*Level 5 Bastion facility*  

- **Prerequisites**: Ability to use a [[Spellcasting Focus]]
- **Space**: Roomy (16 sq)
- **Hirelings**: 1
- **Order**: recruit

Derived from Oikos (household/estate), leaders of Necropoikoi defend their strongholds with Undead (Returned) minions. A Necropoikos can accommodate up to eight Bastion Defenders. 

## Recruit: Returned Defenders

Each time you issue the Recruit action to this facility, up to four Returned Bastion Defenders are created and take shelter in the crypts and burial chambers of your Necropoikos. You can't issue the Recruit order to this facility if it's fully occupied.

Keep track of Returned Bastion Defenders you lose and deduct them from your Bastion Defenders roster. A Returned Bastion Defender returns to your Necropoikos 14 days after being destroyed. Returned Bastion Defenders can't return from being killed if the facility is shut down or fully occupied.

*Source: FRHoF*
